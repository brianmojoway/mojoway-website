Our 7-Day No-Questions-Asked Refund Policy

We want you to be fully satisfied with your MOJOWAY purchase.
At MOJOWAY, we stand behind the quality and value of our self-discovery and coaching tools. If you're not completely happy with your purchase for any reason, we'll give you a full refund — no questions asked — within 7 days of purchase.

Eligibility

The following purchases are eligible for the 7-day no-questions-asked refund policy:

  • The Coach Workspace ($59/month or $590/year) — new subscriptions only
  • Coach Scale ($99/month or $999/year) — new subscriptions only
  • Personal card ($3.90 once) and the optional $9.90/month tracking plan

How to Request a Refund

  1. Contact us by email at support@mojowaylife.com — this opens your mail app with the subject line already filled in.
    Include your order number (found in your confirmation email) or the email address used at checkout.
  2. Keep "Refund Request" in the subject line (the link above adds it for you).
  3. Briefly explain your reason (optional but appreciated). This helps us improve.

Request your refund

Fill this in and we'll open your request with everything in place. No account login needed.

Processing Time

Step Timeline
We acknowledge your request Within 24 hours

What You'll Receive

  • A full refund of your original purchase price
  • No cancellation fees or hidden charges
  • Email confirmation when the refund is processed

Exceptions

The following situations are not eligible for a full refund under this policy:

  • Requests made more than 7 days after purchase
  • For subscription products, if you have already consumed a significant portion of the current billing period's content (e.g., received multiple weeks of daily emails)
  • If you have violated our Terms of Service
  • If we detect fraudulent activity associated with the purchase

In these cases, we will evaluate each situation individually and may offer a partial prorated refund based on usage.

Cancellation of Subscriptions

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your Stripe account dashboard. Upon cancellation:

  • You will retain access to paid content until the end of the current billing period
  • No further charges will be made
  • Cancellations are not retroactive no partial refunds are provided for unused portions of an active subscription period (except as outlined in our 7-day no-questions-asked refund policy above)
