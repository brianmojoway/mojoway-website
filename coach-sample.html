The note you wrote
“Says she's fine in every session, then cancels the week of her big presentation. Sleep goes first. She wants to stop over-preparing but calls the preparation 'just being professional'.”
What the intake asked for
birth date, gender, height and weight. Body composition is optional.

about ten minutes.

1. The meeting framework

Read this in the car on the way in. It is the whole point of the tool.

Open with this question

You call the preparation 'just being professional' — what would it cost to call it something else?

Say this first

The gap between saying she's fine and cancelling the week of her big presentation — that's where her Fire energy meets her running-on-empty body signal.

The bridge — who they are, and how their body is carrying it

As a Connector, you're built to show up for others, but when you're running on empty, your body pulls the plug before you do — sleep goes first, then the cancellation.

Your move in the first ten minutes

In the first 10 minutes, ask her to name one thing she did to rest this week, and one thing she did to prepare. Write both lists side by side and let her see the ratio.

Caution

Don't over-coach her into rest as another performance metric, and don't diagnose her sleep or cancellations as burnout — stay with her words and let her connect the dots.

2. The speed card

Fire · The Connector · born near Minor Heat. The element is the frame; the read below is where it stops being generic.

Why lead with Water

If you lead with Fire — matching her warmth, rallying her, celebrating her output — you feed the exact loop that's exhausting her. Fire over-extended becomes performance: she'll show up brighter, agree faster, and cancel harder. Water is the earned entry because it's the only element that doesn't ask her to produce. Lead wrong here and she'll treat the coaching itself as another thing to over-prepare for — another stage. Lead right and you give her the one experience she's never had: being met without having to be ready.

3. The body read

Written for your awareness, not for the client's file. No numbers are repeated back; nothing here is a health opinion.

On paper this client reads as steady and balanced — the kind of body that looks 'fine' from the outside. But the pattern you're describing tells a different energy story: she holds a composed surface while something underneath runs tight. The cancellations landing right before her big moments, and sleep leaving first, suggest her energy may be spending itself on control and preparation rather than on recovery. 'Just being professional' can feel like a shield — a way to keep the pressure invisible, even to herself. This looks less like a body problem and more like an energy rhythm where output quietly outpaces replenishment.

Ways to put it into words

  • You say you're fine every week — and I believe that's true to you. I'm curious what 'fine' costs you in sleep the week of a big moment.
  • You call the over-preparing 'just being professional.' What if we looked at it as energy spent — and asked whether it's buying you calm or just more tension?
  • Your body seems to keep a very tidy surface while carrying a lot underneath. That's a strength — and it can also mean the strain never gets a voice.

4. The deep read

What is actually going on

A performer who has learned to convert anxiety into productivity — the more she prepares, the more in control she feels, but the control is the drain. The hidden cost: her competence is real, but it's propped up by a private belief that stopping would expose her as ordinary. She doesn't rest because rest feels like a verdict, not a pause.

The stuck point

The belief that rest must be earned by sufficient preparation — so the finish line keeps moving, and stopping always feels premature.

The reframe

You're not preparing to be enough — you're preparing to feel safe. Those are different jobs, and only one of them needs sleep.

How the body shows it

Her body is a kettle left on a low flame — never boiling over, never switched off, quietly evaporating while the surface stays still.

What you can check

Read the deep read again and ask one question: could this describe any client you have? If the answer is yes, do not pay for it. If the answer is no — if you recognised this person in three sentences — that is the whole value of the tool, and the rest is packaging.
