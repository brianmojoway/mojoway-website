The month nothing moved

Client account, recorded October 2026

Month four, nothing moved. Not the weight, not the waist, not the number I'd decided mattered most. Four weeks of doing everything right and the page looked identical to the previous page.

I was ready to stop. I had the sentence prepared and everything.

My coach opened the review and pointed at a row I hadn't looked at: muscle mass, up two kilos, and body fat percentage flat. Same weight, different composition. Which meant the training had worked and the scale had lied, and I'd spent a month feeling like a failure over a number that was never going to move first.

I still remember being annoyed that nobody had told me this in week one. It would have saved me four weeks of private misery.

Now every review has a line at the top about what to expect in this phase, so the quiet months come with a label instead of a verdict.

I didn't quit. I'm in month nineteen and I've since had two more flat months, and both times I went looking for the muscle row instead of the weight.

The document didn't motivate me. It corrected me, which is a different and rarer service.

MOJOWAY's kit puts that context on the page itself, so the explanation doesn't depend on remembering to ask for it.
