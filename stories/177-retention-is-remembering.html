Retention is remembering

Field note, studio operations review

Ask an owner why people leave and you'll hear about price, competition, schedule, life getting in the way. All of those are true and none of them are the mechanism.

The mechanism is simpler. Something was happening for six months, and then the client stopped being able to see it, and then it felt like nothing.

People don't quit things that are visibly working. They quit things they can't measure, and then they explain it with a reason that sounds like a decision.

This is why the most profitable operational change in a small studio isn't a marketing channel. It's a habit of writing things down and handing them over: what the client was, what they are now, what's next, in language they'd use themselves.

A coach who does that is doing retention work every single session without calling it that.

The tools barely matter as long as the record exists. What matters is that the client never has to ask "is this doing anything?" because they were handed the answer at month three.

MOJOWAY's body kit exists to make that record the default output of a session rather than an extra task nobody has time for.
