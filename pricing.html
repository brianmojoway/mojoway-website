Questions people actually ask

Do I have to pay anything today?

Not for your first client — one client is free and needs no card. Past that it is $59/month for 15 client slots ($99/month for 30). A personal card is $3.90 if you want one for yourself, and that is a one-off, not a subscription.

What currency will I be charged in?

Prices are set in US dollars. Stripe may display the equivalent in your local currency depending on where you are paying from; it is the same plan at the same USD price either way. Discount codes and refunds follow the currency you paid in.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time, from your account. There is also a 7-day money-back guarantee with no questions asked, so you can find out whether this fits how you actually work.

What counts as a client slot?

One slot is one client you are working with right now. Coach gives you 15 open at once, Scale gives you 30 — and you can re-run any client as often as you like inside their slot, at no extra cost. Slots free up when you close a client.

Can I use the $3.90 personal card for clients?

No. The personal card is tied to the person who bought it, for their own use. Client cards come from the Coach Workspace, because clients need slots, history and unlimited re-runs — none of which a single one-off card carries.

Is there anything I can look at before deciding?

Yes. The live sample shows the exact report a coach gets back, with no login and nothing stored. There are also two short videos on what changes in your week.
