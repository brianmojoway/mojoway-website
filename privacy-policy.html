Welcome to MOJOWAY. Your privacy matters to us — and we treat your data with the same care we bring to your inner journey. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you stay in control.

1. What We Collect

When you use MOJOWAY (mojowaylife.com), we collect:

  • Information you give us: Your name, email address, and any responses you share in our tools. If you are a coach, the client details you enter (such as a client's date of birth, used to calculate their Five Energies profile on lab.mojowaylife.com) are processed to produce the report you request.
  • Automatically collected data: IP address, browser type, device info, pages visited, and time spent — used to improve your experience.
  • Cookies & similar technologies: We use essential cookies for site function, and optional analytics cookies (with your consent) to understand how you engage with our content.

Health Data: What We Do Not Process

Our public free read does not collect health data. The free read at /body-read.html asks only for a birth date, used once to draw a behaviour-pattern card. We do not ask for weight, height, sex, body measurements or lab values, we do not calculate a body-mass index, and we do not produce a body-composition or wellbeing assessment.

What the read is. A plain-language description of patterns in how you tend to behave. It is not a diagnosis, not a score, not a medical device output, not a prediction, and not a substitute for a clinician. What we call core energy is a behaviour pattern, not a reading of vitality or wellbeing.

Whether we keep anything. The reading is computed in memory and returned to your browser. The birth date and any text you enter are not written to a database, not written to an application log, and not attached to an account. No account is needed for the free read. Standard web-server access logs record the request path, IP address and browser type, but never the values you submit.

Age and location. The free read is for adults aged 18 and over only. We do not offer services to residents of the EU/EEA or UK.

Coach workspace (separate). Coaches using the separate Coach Workspace may enter their own clients' details under their own client relationship and data-processing terms. That is a different tool from the public free read described above.

Personal card & body tool (optional, self-described). The paid Personal card ($3.90) and the body tool at /tool.html are separate from the public free read. They may ask you for a short, self-described note about how you have been feeling or what your body has been doing, and optionally your weight and height. These are optional, self-reported details used only to personalize the written card you receive; they are not a body-fat, composition, wellbeing or health assessment, and we do not derive a body-mass index from them. You can leave every body field blank and still get a card. We do not use these details for advertising, and we do not sell them.

If you are in Washington State or another US state with a consumer health data law: we do not collect consumer health data through the public free read, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.
